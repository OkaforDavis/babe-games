🔠 Word Scramble

How to play Two players take turns. Each round the game secretly picks a word from your chosen category and scrambles it. The player NOT setting the round has to type the correct word before the timer runs out. Score is higher the faster you guess. Roles swap every round.

"Same device" is pass-and-play on one screen. "Play Online" connects you with one other person on a different device using a short room code.

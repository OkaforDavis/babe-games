🌱 Ncho

How to play — capture on four (the main game) Every pit starts with four seeds. On your turn, scoop up one of your pits and drop the seeds one at a time into each pit going round; the houses are only collecting bowls, nothing is sown into them.

Your turn doesn't stop at the end of the handful. If your last seed drops into a pit that already had seeds in it, you scoop that whole pit up — your seed and all — and carry on sowing. You only stop when a seed comes to rest in a pit that was empty.

Any pit that lands on exactly four is lifted out the moment it happens. It goes to whoever owns that pit, so a four you make on your own row is yours and one you make on theirs is theirs. The exception is your final seed: if that's what made the four, you take it wherever it is — and since there's nothing left in your hand, your turn ends there too.

If the player to move has nothing left to sow, the other player takes everything still on the board. Once fewer than four seeds remain nobody can collect again, so it stops there. Most seeds collected wins.

If the seeds just go round with nobody collecting for a very long stretch, the game is called and each player keeps what's on their own side — otherwise it could run forever.
How to play — Mancala / Ayo style The other version. Seeds are sown into your own house as you pass it (never theirs). Land your last seed in an empty pit on your own row and you capture it plus everything in the pit facing it; land it in your house and you go again. When one row empties, the other player sweeps up their own leftovers. Most seeds in your house wins.

🌱 Ncho

How to play (Mancala-style rules) This plays with the widely-known Mancala/Ayo capture rule, since exact house rules for Ncho vary from family to family and estate to estate. Each player owns the row of pits nearest them and the store to their right. On your turn, pick one of your non-empty pits; its seeds are sown one-by-one into each following pit (your own pits, your store, then your opponent's pits — skipping their store). Land your last seed in your own empty pit and you capture it plus everything opposite it, straight into your store. Land it in your store and you go again. When one side is empty, the other keeps whatever's left. Most seeds in your store wins. If this isn't how you play it at home, tell me the house rule and I'll adjust it.

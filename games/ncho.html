🌱 Ncho

How to play Each player owns the six pits on their side and the store at their end. On your turn, pick one of your pits and its seeds are sown one at a time into each pit going round — your pits, your store, then your opponent's pits, never their store. Land your last seed in your own empty pit and you capture it plus everything in the pit facing it. Land it in your own store and you go again. When one side runs out, the other sweeps up what's left. Most seeds in your store wins.

These are the widely-played Mancala/Ayo rules — house rules for Ncho differ from family to family, so tell me yours if it's different.

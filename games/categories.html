🔴 Name, Place, Animal, Thing

How to play Pick a letter. Each player, in turn on this device, gets a fixed time to fill Name, Place, Animal, Thing and Food starting with that letter. When everyone has gone, the group reviews every answer together and the host marks each one valid or not — valid unique answers score more than answers other players also had. Play as many letters as you like.
